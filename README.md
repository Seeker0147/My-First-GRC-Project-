GRC Review: Nexus Financial Services
Case Study Assignment | Lagos, Nigeria
1. What Governance, Risk, and Compliance Mean
Governance: Governance is the way a company is directed and controlled. It includes clear
responsibilities, decision-making processes, policies, and oversight so that people understand who can
make decisions and how those decisions should be made.
Risk Management: Risk management is the process of identifying what could go wrong, assessing how
likely and serious it could be, and taking steps to reduce its impact on the company.
Compliance: Compliance means following the laws, regulations, industry rules, and internal policies that
apply to the company. It also involves monitoring requirements and meeting important deadlines.

3. One Recommendation to Improve the GRC Programme
Recommendation: Establish a simple, company-wide GRC framework, overseen by a named senior
manager or designated GRC lead. The framework should set out who approves and enforces policies,
require a basic risk assessment before significant business decisions, and maintain a central register of
regulatory obligations, responsible owners, and due dates.
This is a practical first step for a growing company that does not yet have separate GRC teams. It creates
clear accountability and consistent processes while helping leadership identify risks early and monitor
compliance obligations.
